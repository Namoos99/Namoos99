# Hi there and welcome to my profile <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MartinHeinz/MartinHeinz/master/wave.gif" width="32px">

I'm Namoos — most people call me Moose. I'm an AI/ML engineer with a master's in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence, drawn to the part of this work where a messy dataset turns into something a person can actually act on.

> "The goal is to turn data into information, and information into insight." — Carly Fiorina, former CEO of Hewlett-Packard

My path here didn't start with a laptop. It started in a room with kids on the autism spectrum 🧩, where I learned to notice the small, repeating signals that tell you what's really going on. I loved that work — and I kept hitting the same wall: I could help the child in front of me, but not the hundred I'd never meet. That's when I understood the difference between helping one person and building something that helps at scale.

The other thread is fashion 👗. A lifelong love that got complicated once I looked at the supply chains behind it. What struck me was that the data already exists — published, disclosed, and read by almost no one. The problem isn't a lack of information. It's that nobody turns it into insight.

Both threads led to the same place: I wanted to make an impact analytically 📊. So I build end to end — pipelines that don't break, systems that fail gracefully, products people can actually use. I'm a woman in STEM, and I'd rather ship the work and let it speak, while leaving the room easier to walk into for whoever comes next.

Outside the terminal, you'll find me kickboxing 🥊 — the best teacher I've had in showing up on the days I don't feel like it — or deep in a sustainable fashion rabbit hole 🌱.

⚡ Fun fact: [I speak 7 languages]
