# Hi there and welcome to my profile 👋

I'm Namoos — most people call me Moose. I'm a data scientist with a master's in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence, and what I care about most is the part of this work where a messy, uncooperative dataset turns into something a person can actually act on.

> "The goal is to turn data into information, and information into insight."
> — Carly Fiorina, former CEO of Hewlett-Packard

That quote is the shortest description of my job I've ever found, and it's the bar I hold myself to. Anyone can produce a number. The harder, more interesting work is producing an answer someone can make a decision with.

My path into this field didn't start with a laptop. It started in a room with kids on the autism spectrum 🧩. That work taught me to pay attention differently — to notice the small, quiet, repeating signals that tell you what's actually going on, long before anyone says it out loud. I loved it deeply. I also kept running into the same wall: I could help the child in front of me, but I couldn't help the hundred I'd never meet. That was the first time I understood the difference between helping one person and building something that helps at scale.

The second thread is fashion 👗. It's been a lifelong love, and the more I learned about the industry, the harder it became to look away from what sits behind the clothes — the supply chains, the labor practices, the environmental cost. What struck me was that so much of this information already exists in the open. It's published, disclosed, sitting in reports nobody reads. The problem isn't a lack of data. It's that nobody has turned it into insight.

Those two threads met in the same conclusion: I wanted to make an impact analytically 📊. That's what pulled me toward data science and AI — not the models for their own sake, but the engineering underneath them. Pipelines that don't break. Systems that fail gracefully. Products people can actually use, including the people usually designed around instead of designed for. I build end to end because insight that never reaches anyone isn't insight yet.

I'm a woman in STEM, and I hold that with intention. I'd rather build the thing, ship it, and let the work speak — while leaving the room a little easier to walk into for whoever comes next.

Outside of the terminal, you'll find me in the ring — I kickbox 🥊, and it's the best teacher I've had in showing up on the days I don't feel like it. Turns out debugging at 11pm and finishing the last round use the same muscle. I'm also endlessly curious about sustainable fashion 🌱, and I'll happily talk your ear off about it.

I believe the best work sits where technical depth meets something you actually care about. That's where I try to build.
